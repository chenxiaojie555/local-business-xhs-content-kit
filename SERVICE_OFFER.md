# Service Offer

## Local Business REDNote Content Trial

I help local businesses turn hard-ad-style shop information into REDNote / Xiaohongshu-ready content drafts.

This is useful when a shop has products, photos, and a real location, but does not know how to write posts that feel native to the platform.

## Trial Package

Deliverables:

- Quick content audit
- One complete post draft
- Three title options
- Three cover copy options
- Keywords / hashtags
- Posting notes

Typical turnaround: 24-48 hours after receiving shop information and images.

## Three-Post Package

Deliverables:

- Three complete post drafts
- Title and cover copy options for each post
- Keywords / hashtags for each post
- One-week publishing rhythm
- One light revision

## Monthly Basic Package

Deliverables:

- Twelve post drafts per month
- Weekly keyword / topic suggestions
- Lightweight performance review
- Monthly content direction update

## What I Need From The Client

- Shop name
- City / district / business area
- Main product or service
- Target customers
- Price range
- Existing social links, if any
- Three to five real shop or product images
- Any compliance-sensitive claims to avoid

## What I Do Not Do

- No fake reviews
- No fake followers
- No spam outreach
- No guaranteed viral claims
- No account verification bypass
- No publishing through client accounts unless separately agreed and authorized
