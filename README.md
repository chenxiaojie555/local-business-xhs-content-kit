# Local Business REDNote / Xiaohongshu Content Kit

AI-assisted content workflow for local businesses that want to start with REDNote / Xiaohongshu without hiring a full agency.

This repository is a public portfolio/demo kit. It contains reusable SOPs, outreach scripts, pricing logic, lead screening templates, and one sample delivery package.

## Quick Demo

Use the kit to sell a small paid trial:

1. Find a local shop whose content is too ad-like or outdated.
2. Send a short personalized audit message.
3. Ask for shop name, location, product/service, target customer, and photos.
4. Deliver one native-style post draft plus title and cover copy options.
5. Offer a three-post package or monthly basic package after the trial.

## What This Is

Many local businesses already have good products, but their social content still looks like hard advertising: product photos, price, address, and little user context.

This kit turns that into a lightweight service workflow:

- Audit a local shop's current content.
- Rewrite the angle from business-first to user-first.
- Deliver one REDNote-style post draft.
- Provide title options, cover copy, keywords, and posting notes.
- Use a small paid trial before selling a monthly package.

## Who It Helps

Best-fit business types:

- Beauty salons and nail studios
- Pet stores and pet hospitals
- Cafes, tea shops, bakeries, and light restaurants
- Photography studios
- Education and tutoring services, with compliance caution

## Service Version

The demo can be turned into paid service packages:

- Trial package: one content audit, one full post draft, three title options, three cover copy options.
- Three-post package: three post drafts plus one-week publishing rhythm.
- Monthly basic package: weekly content production and lightweight data review.

No fake reviews, fake followers, spam outreach, or guaranteed viral claims.

## How To Use

1. Pick one local industry.
2. Use `templates/lead-screening-table.csv` to shortlist shops.
3. Use `templates/outreach-scripts.zh-CN.md` to send one-to-one messages.
4. Collect business information and images from the client.
5. Deliver a package like `examples/milk-tea-shop-rednote-delivery.zh-CN.md`.
6. Review content performance and offer a next package.
